# Epic Games Store Requirements & Guidelines

> Source: [dev.epicgames.com](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/epic-games-store/requirements-guidelines/distribution-requirements/requirements-overview)

## Overview

Requirements and guidelines for publishing on the Epic Games Store.

## Access

- Must be **18 years or older** to use EGS publishing tools

## Technical Requirements

### Cross-Platform Multiplayer (PC)

Products with online multiplayer **must support crossplay across all PC storefronts** where distributed. Options:
- Epic Online Services Crossplay
- Proprietary solution
- Third-party system

> Crossplay is not mandatory for ALL multiplayer games — only when distributed on multiple PC storefronts.

### Achievements

If your product has achievements on other PC storefronts, it **must also support EGS achievements** with substantially similar content.

Exceptions:
- Web-based / HTML products
- Early Access titles (may delay until official release)

### Blockchain / NFT / Crypto

Products supporting blockchain tech may be distributed **if they adhere to Epic's blockchain policies**. Non-compliance prevents publication.

### Patch Notes

- Optional at initial launch, recommended for updates
- Must be in English
- Include version identifier + description
- Max **2000 characters** with formatting support

## Store Presence Requirements

### Promotional Content

Must disclose paid advertisements, product placements, logos, or brand promotions. Triggers a "promotional content" label on storefront.

### In-App Purchase Transparency (PC)

Products on Windows/macOS with in-app purchases must include FAQ entries covering:
1. Whether gameplay items are purchasable
2. Whether the product functions without purchases
3. Refund availability and policies
4. Payment platform used (Epic Games or third-party)

> Mobile-only products don't require FAQ pages.

### Terms & Conditions

Products requiring EULA/ToS consent must:
- Link documents from the product page
- Host in-product terms publicly
- Link from storefront listing

## Content Ratings

### IARC Ratings

Epic Games Store uses the **International Age Rating Coalition (IARC)** system. Products must have appropriate ratings before release.

### User-Generated Content

UGC in your product is treated as **unrated (mature 18+)**. You are responsible for moderating UGC.

## Pre-Purchase

EGS supports pre-purchase for unreleased products. Follow Epic's pre-purchase guidelines and best practices.

## Early Access

The store welcomes Early Access releases for in-development products gathering user feedback.

## Ecom Interface

The Ecom Interface enables in-game purchases through EGS. **Online ownership verification on trusted servers is strongly recommended** to prevent piracy.

## Content Guidelines Summary

| Area | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Accuracy | Store page must accurately represent product |
| Age ratings | IARC rating required |
| Prohibited content | Hateful, illegal, exploitative material banned |
| External links | Must be consistent with store page content |
| Localization | English metadata required; additional languages optional |
| DRM | Not required by Epic |
| Blockchain | Allowed with policy compliance |
| UGC | Treated as mature; developer must moderate |
